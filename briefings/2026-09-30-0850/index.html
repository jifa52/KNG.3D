Reuters Singapore reported Saudi Arabia has resumed oil loadings from Yanbu after restarting the East-West Pipeline. Two trade sources estimated Yanbu loadings at about 2 million barrels a day since last week; Kpler puts pipeline throughput around 2.65 million barrels a day and expects 3–4 million soon; a full return to ~5.5 million pre-attack could take another month. Aramco notified customers Monday evening of its October Yanbu schedule, an Asian source said. TankerTrackers cited a 27 September satellite of ~10 million barrels loading. Aramco did not respond. The rate ladder sits below yesterday’s Bloomberg report of at least about 3.5 million barrels a day — different sources, and not a single official figure. Aramco has not put a rate on the record.
