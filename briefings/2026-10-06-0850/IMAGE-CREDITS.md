## Support — Yanbu industrial city, 1 December 2013

- File: `assets/images/yanbu-industrial-1600.jpg` (1600×900), `assets/images/yanbu-industrial-960.jpg` (960×540)
- What it shows: Yanbu industrial city on the Red Sea, photographed 1 December 2013. Yanbu is the western end of the East-West route.
- Why it is not the reports: the story is a conflict between an AFP source who said pumping had stopped after a claimed attack on a Khurais station, and Reuters and Bloomberg sources who said oil was still flowing, plus a Houthi claim of strikes on Rabigh and Riyadh airport. This is Yanbu in 2013. It is not Khurais, not a photograph of flows stopping, not Rabigh, and not King Khalid airport.
