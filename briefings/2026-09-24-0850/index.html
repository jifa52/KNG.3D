Reuters (SINGAPORE/NEW DELHI, Sept 22 — Yap/Verma/Tan; “September 22, 2026 10:08 AM UTC” / Updated September 23, 2026): Saudi Arabia has restarted operations at its East-West Pipeline (צינור מזרח–מערב) and could resume exports from Red Sea port Yanbu (ינבוע), three sources briefed on the matter said. The pipeline is pumping at a low rate after the restart, two sources said. Aramco (אראמקו) is seeking to get pumping back to ~4 million barrels a day (חביות ליום / mbpd; pre-shutdown Hormuz-bypass (עקיפת הורמוז) flow, about 4% of global supply); line capacity is 7 million barrels a day. A security source: ~40% of capacity in a couple of days; a full restart in 6 to 8 weeks. A separate oil-industry source: up to about six weeks to full rates. Context as Reuters carried: drone attacks that Saudi Arabia blamed on an Iraqi militia forced an 11 September shutdown and halted Yanbu loadings; three pumping stations were damaged (satellite + industry sources); one Yanbu cargo was scheduled to load later Tuesday bound for China (a source). Aramco had no immediate comment.

The write-up: 7–14 ms decisions, under 1GB RAM, zero cloud; about 50x faster than the original Python; Snake demo at about 60 decisions/sec; choice / score / yes-no in one call; CoreML version cuts power by up to about 64%. Install: pip install laya-mlx; Hugging Face checkpoints.
