Reuters Singapore Sept 18: preliminary Kpler at 02:00 GMT — four commodity vessels transited the Strait of Hormuz (מיצר הורמוז) Thursday (three in, one out), down from six a day earlier, below a 10-day average of about 16. Mix: two Panamax (פנמקס), one Supramax (סופרמקס), one Kamsarmax (קמסרמקס). Figures could change: some ships switch off transponders (משדרים). Separately, three LNG (גז טבעי נוזלי) carriers reappeared outside the strait Thursday (Al Daayen, Al Samriya, Marigold LNG). Bab el-Mandeb (מיצר באב אל-מנדב): 23 Thursday vs ~26 ten-day average.
